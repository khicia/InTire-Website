01 / THE MISSION

Confidence starts
with a closer look.

InTIRE brings tire awareness closer to drivers by making visible tire inspection more approachable and easier to understand.

Why InTIRE exists

A small check today can make a meaningful difference on the road.

Damage can be easy to miss during a quick walk-around. InTIRE is designed to help users notice possible concerns and decide when to seek professional help.

LOOK. SCAN. GO.

The challenge

Not every tire problem is obvious.

Manual inspection takes time and experience. Drivers may not notice cracks, bulges, punctures, or tread wear until a problem interrupts a trip.

03 / DETECTION FOCUS

Spot the signs.

InTIRE focuses on visible tire conditions. Lighting, image quality, dirt, and other obstructions may affect detection.

⌁

Cracks

Surface or sidewall cracking that may signal tire aging or damage.

⌒

Bulges

Raised areas that can indicate a possible weakness in the tire structure.

⊕

Punctures

Visible holes or foreign objects that may lead to air loss.

▥

Worn treads

Visible tread wear that may reduce grip and needs closer assessment.
