What is InTIRE?

Background

Worn treads and hairline sidewall damage are hard to catch by eye, and most drivers only think about their tires after something goes wrong. Blowouts and skidding tied to poor tire condition remain a preventable cause of road accidents.

The problem

Manual tire inspection takes trained eyes and time most drivers don't have. Vulcanizing shops are often visited only after a puncture — not before one.

Our purpose

InTIRE puts that inspection in your pocket. A phone camera and a deep learning model trained on real tire damage do the looking, so drivers get an early, honest read on tire health.

InTIRE features

Everything you need to keep an eye on tire condition, in one app.

Scanner

Guided camera capture lines up each tire for a clean, consistent scan.

AI detection

A CNN and YOLOv8 model classify damage type and location in seconds.

DOT code scanner

Scan the sidewall's DOT code to pull tire size, manufacturer, and manufacture date.

Health report

Every scan becomes a plain-language health score with a clear next step.

Scan history

Track how each tire changes over time and revisit past results anytime.

Nearby shops

Find nearby vulcanizing shops and service centers when a fix can't wait.

What can InTIRE detect?

Cracks

Surface and sidewall cracking from age or dry rot.

Bulges

Weak spots where internal layers have separated.

Punctures

Foreign objects and holes that lead to slow leaks or blowouts.

Worn treads

Tread depth below the safe threshold for road grip.

How it works

  1. 1

    Scan

    Open the app and capture each tire with your camera.

  2. 2

    Analyze

    The image runs through InTIRE's trained detection model.

  3. 3

    Detect

    Cracks, bulges, punctures, or worn treads are flagged and located.

  4. 4

    Results

    Get a health score and a plain-language recommendation.
